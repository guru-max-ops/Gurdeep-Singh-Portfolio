How I Work

A structured build loop that keeps speed useful and quality defensible.

  1. 01

    Understand the actual requirement

    Translate requirements into the real surface area: UI, states, integrations, permissions, edge cases, and delivery constraints.

  2. 02

    Turn scope into buildable tasks

    Break the work into components, routes, flows, contracts, and implementation milestones that can actually be executed cleanly.

  3. 03

    Use AI strategically

    Leverage AI, Codex, Antigravity, and automation workflows to accelerate the right parts of the build without outsourcing judgment.

  4. 04

    Maintain product and architecture guardrails

    Keep auth, role behavior, state flow, and integration logic aligned while refining the interface and interaction layer.

  5. 05

    Verify before finalizing

    Check the result against the actual requirement, inspect edge behavior, and tighten the final output before it gets handed over.
